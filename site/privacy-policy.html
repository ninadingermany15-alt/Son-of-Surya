Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

We respect your privacy and collect only what we need. This policy explains what we collect, where it is kept, how we use it, and your rights under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What data we collect

When you order, we collect your name, email, phone number and, for physical products, your delivery address. When you download a free guide, join a waitlist, sign up for the newsletter, volunteer, enquire about partnering or use the contact form, we collect the details you type into that form. We do not create user accounts or store passwords. If you chat with Suryaputra, our support assistant, your messages stay in your browser for that visit; if you choose to continue on WhatsApp, the summary you send goes to us through WhatsApp.

How we use your data

We use your data to confirm and deliver orders, email paid guides, reply to your questions, send the newsletter (only if you opted in) and improve the website. Free guides carry your name as a personal watermark. We do not sell or rent your data.

Data sharing

Form entries are stored in a private Google Sheet owned by Son of Surya (Google acts as our storage provider). We share delivery details only with the courier or supplier who ships your order. Payments are made directly through your own UPI app; we never see or store your card or bank details. The website is hosted on Cloudflare Pages.

Cookies

We use your browser's local storage to keep your cart, your chat with Suryaputra and your cookie choice. These are needed for the site to work. We do not run advertising trackers. If we add analytics, it will be privacy-friendly and only with your consent.

Data retention

We retain order data for 7 years as required by Indian tax law. Newsletter subscriber data is retained until you unsubscribe. Contact form submissions are retained for 2 years. You may request deletion of your data at any time (subject to legal retention requirements).

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, email sonofsurya15@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Security

We keep the Google Sheet private, use two-step sign-in on our accounts and limit who can see your data. No internet transmission is completely secure, so please email us straight away if you think something is wrong.

Design Studio, maps and payments

Designs you make in the Surya Design Studio are stored only in your own browser unless you download them. To show maps and weather the studio sends the location you choose to: Esri (satellite imagery), OpenStreetMap Nominatim and Overpass (address search and building outlines) and NASA POWER (climate data). It does not send your name or email to them. When you buy a plan or a guide outside India, your payment is handled by our payment partners (Paddle and/or PayPal), who process your card or account details under their own privacy policies; we receive your name, email, country and the items bought. To activate a plan we store your email, plan and renewal date in our order sheet and send you a one-time code by email.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. We will notify newsletter subscribers of significant changes.

Privacy questions? Email sonofsurya15@gmail.com.
