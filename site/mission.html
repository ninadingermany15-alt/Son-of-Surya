Why rural India first

Rural India: strong sun and a gap to close

Most of India has strong sun for most of the year, yet villages still live with power cuts, diesel pumps and kerosene lamps. Heat, dust, monsoons and salt air make ordinary solar fail early, few installers design for them, and village families pay the price in rusted frames and dead inverters.

Rural India is also where young people leave home for the cities because work in the village is thin. Clean energy, water and farm businesses can change that, if they are built and run locally.

We start with the villages that need it most, prove the model honestly, then grow across India.

What we hold ourselves to

Basic needs first

Light, water, income and skills before anything fancy. A village should not need to move to a city to live well.

Local people do the work

Every project trains and pays people from the village. Outsiders teach, then step back.

Honest numbers, published

Conservative estimates, open assumptions, and an impact log that shows what failed as well as what worked.

Partners, not donors

We ask companies, NGOs and well-off families for their time and skills as much as their money.
