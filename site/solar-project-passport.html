Delivered in 5–7 working days · DISCOM-ready documents

The Engineer-Verified Solar Project Passport is our most complete service. It goes beyond the Free Solar Passport with a site-specific design, DISCOM-ready electrical drawings, SLD, shadow analysis and datasheets, prepared and checked by a solar engineer using professional design software and real satellite data.

Our Quality Promise

Every Passport is prepared using professional solar design software and real satellite and irradiance data, checked by a solar engineer, and made to Indian standards (CEA, MNRE, BIS). If your DISCOM asks for changes to our design, we revise it free of charge.

What Your Passport Includes

Complete electrical planset — all drawings required for DISCOM/MEDA submission
Single Line Diagram (SLD) showing the complete electrical layout
Bill of Materials (BOM) with product specifications and indicative pricing
Shadow analysis — identifying shading objects and their seasonal impact
Product datasheets for all major components
Installation and maintenance guide for your system
Approximate project quotation to compare with EPC quotes
Roof layout and panel placement design from satellite map or your photos
Solar irradiance and generation estimate for your exact location
Full cost and ROI calculation with payback period
Subsidy eligibility check (PM Surya Ghar, PM-KUSUM, state schemes)
Government-compliant product recommendations (ALMM-listed modules, DCR panels where required, BIS-certified components)

This is a preliminary pre-design report based on satellite imagery, customer-provided photos, and the information you supply. The final design requires a physical site visit by a qualified solar engineer.

Where required by your DISCOM, final submission documents must be signed by a licensed electrical contractor.
