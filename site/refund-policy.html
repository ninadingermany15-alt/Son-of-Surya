Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

We want you to be satisfied with your purchase. Please read this policy carefully before buying.

Physical products — returns

Physical products can be returned within 7 days of delivery if they arrive damaged, defective, or significantly different from the product description. To initiate a return, email sonofsurya15@gmail.com with your order number and clear photographs of the issue. We do not accept returns for change of mind.

Physical products — refunds

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we refund you within 5–7 working days to the UPI ID or bank account you paid from. Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the problem was our mistake.

Digital products — no refunds

Paid digital guides cannot be returned once they have been emailed, because a file cannot be given back. If a file is broken, missing pages or not the guide you ordered, email us within 7 days and we will send the right file or refund you.

Solar Project Passport

The Solar Project Passport service fee is refundable only before work on the report begins. Once our team has started the pre-design work, no refund can be issued. If you wish to cancel before work begins, email sonofsurya15@gmail.com with the subject 'Passport Cancellation' and your order details.

Surya Design Studio plans and Project Pass

Pro and Business plans are prepaid for one month or one year and end on their own unless you renew; we email you a week before the end. Where a plan is set up as an automatic subscription instead, it renews until you cancel, and you can cancel at any time from the link in your receipt email or by writing to sonofsurya15@gmail.com. Either way your plan stays active until the end of the period you have paid for, and we do not refund part-periods. If you are not happy with your first payment for a plan or a Project Pass, ask within 14 days and we will refund it in full. Payments made outside India are processed by PayPal (or our reseller and payment partner where shown at checkout); refunds go back to the same payment method.

Buyers in the European Union

Our guides and studio plans are digital content and services. When you ask us to start delivery straight away (download links or immediate access), you agree that your 14-day right of withdrawal ends once delivery has begun. Our own 14-day money-back promise for first plan payments above still applies.

Cancellations

Orders for physical products can be cancelled within 12 hours of placement, provided they have not yet been dispatched. To cancel, email sonofsurya15@gmail.com with your order number. Once dispatched, cancellations are not possible — the return process applies instead.

Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges at this time. If you receive a defective or incorrect item, we will send a replacement after the return is processed.

How to contact us

For all return and refund queries, email sonofsurya15@gmail.com with your order number, a description of the issue, and photographs where applicable. We aim to respond within 1–2 business days.

Questions? Email sonofsurya15@gmail.com with your order number.
