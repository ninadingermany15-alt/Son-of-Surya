Agrivoltaics · Rural India

Where the sun meets the earth

Solar power and farming on the same land. Raised panels make clean electricity while crops keep growing in their shade, for farms and villages across rural India.

Design your solar, free

From sunlight to a village that never goes dark

Energy path · 4 stages
01

SUNLIGHT

Sunlight reaches the photovoltaic modules. Much of India gets strong sun for most of the year, which makes every rooftop and every field a power source.

Peak irradiance
≈ 1,000 W/m²
Peak sun hours
4–6 h/day, India
02

SOLAR GENERATION

Monocrystalline cells turn that light into direct current. Raised 3–4 metres above the field, the panels share the land with the crops below.

Module efficiency
21–22 %
Daily yield
≈ 4 kWh per kWp
03

ENERGY CONVERSION

Cables carry the power through combiner boxes to the inverter, which turns it into alternating current for the pumps, homes and the grid.

DC → AC
Inverter, 230 / 415 V out
Inverter efficiency
97–98 %
04

POWERING COMMUNITIES

The energy runs irrigation pumps, farm equipment and village homes. What is left over flows into the grid under net metering.

Uses
Pumps · homes · streetlights
Surplus
Exported via net meter
