Building the Son of Surya community

Son of Surya is at the beginning of its journey. Genuine customer experiences and project stories will be published here as the community grows — with full permission from each contributor.

Transparent Methodology

Every calculation and estimate clearly states its assumptions and limitations. We never hide how we arrive at a number.

Structured Solar Education

Guides are built around a logical learning path — from understanding solar basics to evaluating EPC quotations and applying for subsidies.

Official-Source Subsidy Information

Subsidy details are sourced directly from MNRE, PM Surya Ghar, and state DISCOM portals — not third-party summaries.

Practical Checklists

Every guide includes actionable checklists so you know exactly what to do, what to ask, and what to watch out for.

Clear Assumptions & Limitations

Our tools provide estimates and guidance. We are clear about where professional verification or a site survey is still required.

Founder Transparency

Son of Surya is built by engineers in renewable energy, in public. No hidden team, no exaggerated credentials.

India-First Approach

Every guide, tool, and product recommendation is designed for Indian conditions — dust, heat, monsoons, voltage fluctuations, and the Indian subsidy landscape.

Free Design Studio for Everyone

Any homeowner, farmer or student can design a real solar system on their own roof, with satellite view, 3D shading and NASA climate data, at no cost. Installers who need their own branding pay, so the tool stays free for families.
