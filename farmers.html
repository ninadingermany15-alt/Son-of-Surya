PM-KUSUM

What the scheme covers, and where it stands

  • Pumps (B and C): usually central 30%, state at least 30%, farmer at most 40% of the benchmark or tender cost, whichever is lower. North-eastern and hill states: central 50%, farmer at most 20%.
  • Timeline: MNRE has said projects under the current phase must be completed by 31 March 2027, and it is finalising PM-KUSUM 2.0. We update our guides when the new rules are notified.
  • Where to apply: only through your state’s official nodal agency or DISCOM portal; ask your agriculture office which one runs it in your state. Never through a website that asks for a “registration fee”.

Beware fake KUSUM websites and agents

  • Government portals end in .gov.in or .nic.in.
  • No official scheme asks you to pay a registration fee to a personal account.
  • “Pay ₹45,000 today, 90% subsidy” on the spot is a warning sign. Check first.
